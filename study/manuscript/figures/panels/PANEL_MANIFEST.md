# Panel files of Figures 1 to 5 and S1 to S8

41 panels, each as a 600 dpi PNG file, in this folder. The composite figures (PDF, SVG, PNG and TIFF) are in the folder above (`figures/`). Each panel file holds its axes, its legend (a legend that the composite shares across panels is repeated in every panel that uses it) and its title; the panel letter and the caption are left out. Width is 85 mm (single column) or 180 mm (wide panels); Arial 7 pt as in the composites, except the panels with a larger text size given in the table (all text scaled by one factor). Figure 1 and the figures with a single panel (S3, S7, S8) are one file each.

Written by `scripts/figures/build_all.py`: each panel is drawn by the function of its composite, run in panel mode. The caption column is the caption text that belongs to the panel (the text shared by the whole figure, then the text of the panel). Sizes are those stored when the panel was drawn. Every panel is drawn from one of the composites, and the numbers in the SVG text of the composites are checked by `scripts/analysis/check_figure_bindings.py` against the bound values; the panels are not checked separately.
